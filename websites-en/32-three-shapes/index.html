What You Will Do

Phase 01

Observe and Analyze

Dissect seminal Bauhaus publications to grasp how negative whitespace, visual weight, and directional vectors establish information hierarchy. Analyze asymmetric equilibrium and primary color theory.

Focus: Golden ratio grids, geometric counterweights, visual deconstruction
Phase 02

Hands-on Cut & Paste

Step away from digital screens. Work with heavyweight art paper in pure primaries, T-squares, precision scissors, and paste to physically feel the kinetic gravity of each shape on an A3 canvas.

Materials: 300gsm art papers, cutting mats, precision scissors, T-squares
Phase 03

Compare and Refine

Pin works side by side onto the studio critique board. Discuss visual tension directly with peers, adjusting angles and positions to discover the optimal point of balanced visual force.

Deliverables: 3 finished A3 geometric poster compositions crafted entirely with your own hands
