What Happens During the Session

Step by step, grounding every sense

Never rushed, never pressured. Every moment at Thảo An is crafted with mindful attentiveness for your utmost ease.

01

The Welcome

Stepping through the doorway, you are greeted by the gentle aroma of lemongrass and a warm cup of roasted black bean tea. A mindful interlude to slow your heartbeat and leave noise behind before treatment begins.

Clean botanical essence: Crisp lemongrass essential oil paired with warm roasted black bean and dried lotus petal infusion.
02

The Experience

In ambient lighting and soothing acoustic melodies, the practitioner begins your treatment. You hold full freedom to request pressure adjustments—gentler or firmer—or shift focus areas to feel completely at ease. Care always follows your natural rhythm.

Attentive adjustments: You can request deeper or lighter pressure, or adjust the warmth of herbal compresses at any moment.
03

The Conclusion

As the treatment concludes, we preserve private space for you to gently awaken your senses. You will be served candied ginger and warm water to stabilize energy, carrying a lingering feeling of weightlessness as you depart.

Gentle awakening: Organic waffle linen towel, warming ginger slice, and infused warm water to balance your equilibrium.

Before You Arrive

Mindful reminders for your session

To ensure your experience unfolds smoothly and gently, here are a few simple recommendations from Thảo An.

  • Arrive 10 minutes early: Sip a warm cup of roasted black bean tea and ground your energy before entering the room.
  • Wear loose clothing: Choose breathable, relaxed apparel so your muscles can release fully without constriction.
  • Enjoy a light meal 1 hour prior: Avoid being overly full or completely hungry during your bodywork session.
  • Honor the quiet sanctuary: Keep mobile phones silenced to preserve peace and deep stillness for all.
