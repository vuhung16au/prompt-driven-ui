Clear Scope of Service

Transparency in Every Item

We perform bookkeeping and periodic reporting based on the documents you provide. This does not include unproven penalty compensation commitments or unofficial legal advice.

In Scope

  • ✦ Verify validity of input invoices and documents.
  • ✦ Record data into accounting software.
  • ✦ Prepare periodic tax reports and annual financial statements.

Out of Scope

  • ✦ Consulting on tax evasion or forging documents.
  • ✦ Commitments to pass tax audits or compensate for penalties not caused by accounting errors.
  • ✦ Digital signatures, e-invoices (equipped by client).
