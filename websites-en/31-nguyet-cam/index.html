Architecture & Acoustics

The Cabaret Hall

Rooted in the 1920s golden age of European chamber salons, Nguyệt Cầm is adorned with hand-cast brass embellishments, dark polished timber, and sound-absorbing velvet drapery.

The hall’s acoustic envelope is carefully calibrated for authentic, warm unamplified sound, avoiding artificial electronic harshness.
