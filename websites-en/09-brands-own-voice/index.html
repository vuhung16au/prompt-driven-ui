Identity & Website

Moc Crafts

Problem & Requirement

The client needed an identity system and website that evokes a handcrafted, rustic feel while maintaining elegance and sophistication. The brand wanted to emphasize materials and the crafting process.

Design Decisions

We decided to use paper texture as the visual foundation, combined with handcrafted serif typography. The image rhythm is paced slowly, focusing on product details.

Role: Identity Design, Interface Design (UI/UX).
Deliverables: Logo system, Brand Guidelines, and Responsive Website.
