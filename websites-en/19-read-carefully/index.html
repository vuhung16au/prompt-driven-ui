Syllabus

Four Core Chapters

Volume I

Read the Question

Result: Accurately grasp the core issue the text attempts to answer before analyzing content.

Identify the main question in a policy text. [Sample exercise]

Volume II

Separate the Arguments

Result: Deconstruct the text into smaller arguments, identifying premises and conclusions.

Break down a newspaper editorial into an argument tree. [Sample exercise]

Volume III

Verify the Evidence

Result: Evaluate the reliability, relevance, and sufficiency of the provided evidence.

Assess 3 statistical data sources in an annual report. [Sample exercise]

Volume IV

Write the Rebuttal

Result: Restate your argument coherently, refuting or agreeing with a solid foundation.

Write a 200-word rebuttal paragraph for the analyzed essay. [Sample exercise]

The Discussion Facilitator

(Illustrative persona and photo)

The facilitator's role is not to lecture one-way, but to create a space for you to reflect and debate.

We will dissect complex texts together, ask hard questions, and hone critical thinking through each session. The facilitator acts as a guide, helping you see the blind spots in arguments you might have missed.
