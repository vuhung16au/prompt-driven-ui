## Typography

Inter 400/500/600/700. H1 desktop 72 px/1.1, tablet 52 px, mobile 38 px; step numbers 64 px desktop/40 px mobile; panel title 36 px/1.2; body 18 px/1.6. Step numbers must not be so large as to push the step's deliverables off-screen.

## Grid, Rhythm, and Responsiveness

Max-width 1200 px, desktop panel 6 columns image/6 columns content, gap 32 px; header area outside the stack. Panels must not be taller than viewport minus navigation and 24 px margin. When content overflows or viewport height is under 700 px, switch to normal document flow.

## Surfaces and Materials

Radius 12 px, border 1 px, shadow 0 12 px 32 px rgba(30,40,28,.10). Stack offset 24 px, scale from .96 to 1; depth is created using actual DOM cards, not overlapping screenshots. Z-index increases sequentially with step order; focus must not get trapped in occluded panels.

## Components and States

### Step Selector

Buttons 1–4 with step name labels; aria-current for the active step. Selecting a button scrolls to the section without scrolljacking.

### Step Panel

Consists of inputs, tasks/execution, deliverables/outputs, and imagery. Do not allow important CTAs at the bottom of the panel to be clipped. Content must exist directly in the DOM.

### View All

Disable sticky/transform while preserving content and sequence; focus is returned to the heading of the step currently being viewed.

### Handover Checklist

Clear checkbox labels, state stored within the test session; do not auto-check as completed simply due to scrolling past a step. Resetting does not alter the viewport mode.

## Motion and Reduced-Motion Strategy

Based on natural scroll progress, do not intercept wheel events. Maximum scale 4%, no opacity 0. Reduced-motion and mobile switch to static flow; motion is not a prerequisite for reading content.

## Things to Avoid

Prohibited: pinning every section, artificial total height that makes scrolling excessively long, focus trapped beneath occluding panels, applying the identical effect across 37 templates, or mutating suitable content merely to hook up n8n.

## Pre-deployment Validation

Measure contrast for text/surface pairs and actual interactive states; do not rely on standalone hex color values to claim compliance. Verify full Vietnamese diacritics and fallback fonts. The content layout, dataset, cover, and dedicated scenarios follow immediately after this brief in the standalone prompt.
