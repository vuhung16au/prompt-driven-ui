3. Frosted Glass Specification
Glass: night-blue background #0b1024, soft blue/teal halos, light text. Semi-transparent glass surface with subtle light borders, moderate blur, and soft shadows to convey depth. Hero features a crisp, real product photo alongside the message; glass is used to contain concise information, not to blur over the product itself. Feature, usage, and selection sections follow the project data. Primary buttons are solid color for easy discovery. Maintain a sufficiently dark fallback background when blur is not supported; do not blur the entire page. Mobile reduces effects and stacks vertically.
Detail to preserve: Use multi-layered soft shadows, do not use a single flat shadow — that is what makes frosted glass look cheap.
Note upon implementation: A solid fallback background is mandatory. Any device with reduced transparency enabled without a solid fallback will have its text disappear.

## Typography

Inter 400/500/600. H1 desktop 60 px/1.1, tablet 46 px/1.15, mobile 36 px/1.2. H2 28 px; item title 18 px/1.35; body 16 px/1.6; metadata 14 px/1.4. Do not reduce text opacity just to make the material look more transparent.

## Grid, Rhythm, and Responsive

Desktop max-width 1200 px, calendar 8 columns and detail 4 columns; gap 24 px. 768 px moves agenda and detail into the same column. 390 px gutter 16–20 px, inputs full-width. Background image must have low-detail areas behind the timeline; background-position must be locked to a specific crop.

## Surface and Material

Backdrop blur 20 px and saturation 120%; white panel opacity .82, highlight inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.65), shadow 0 16px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.12). Radius 20 px. Only two visible glass layers; modal/entry forms use opacity .96 to ensure legibility. Do not blur text content directly.

## Components and States

### Calendar Panel

Calendar on glass surface, text on a separate layer, background behind does not click-through. Slot has only a single row of labels; selected state uses both fill and checkmark.

### Text Field

Label above field, nearly solid background, border 1 px, height 48 px. Focus 2 px #075B91, error has subtext and aria-describedby. Placeholder is only an example, not a replacement for label.

### Popover

Do not apply nested backdrop blur. Use a nearly solid background and dedicated z-index, with sufficient spacing so as not to obscure the active control.

### Reduced Transparency

Toggle has visible label, proper aria-pressed. Switches all panels to surface-solid, without changing data or layout. If backdrop-filter is unsupported, use this fallback automatically.

## Motion and Reduced Motion Alternatives

Panel fade/translate 4 px in 180 ms, without making the background drift with the cursor. Reduced-motion removes translate; reduced transparency is an independent option, not to be equated with reduced motion.

## Things to Avoid

Prohibited: light text over light image areas, heavily nested multiple blur layers, neon shadows, background images with busy details clashing with text, or calling this an official Apple Liquid Glass implementation.

## Pre-implementation Value Checks

Measure contrast between text/surface pairs and actual states; do not rely on isolated hex colors to claim compliance. Check for full Vietnamese diacritics support and font fallbacks.
