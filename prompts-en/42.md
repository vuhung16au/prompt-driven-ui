# High-Fidelity Modern-Retro Design System

## Design Philosophy

**Core Concept: Editorial Nostalgia**
This design system bridges the gap between mid-century print magazines, vintage apothecaries, and ultra-modern digital minimalism. It feels like a high-end, tactile printed brochure or a premium artisanal product label brought to life on screen. It rejects the bubbly, rounded trends of modern UI in favor of sharp lines, high contrast, and unapologetic typographic scale.

**The "Modern-Retro" Difference**:
Unlike purely vintage designs that rely on heavy textures, sepia tones, or distressing, **Modern-Retro** is clean and crisp. It uses vintage *proportions* (condensed display fonts, grid-based layouts, thin rules) but renders them with modern digital precision.

* **Materiality**: Think of high-quality matte paper, foil-stamped labels, and rich, deeply saturated inks. The screen should feel flat but premium.
* **Lighting**: Flat. There are no simulated light sources, no bevels, and no drop shadows. Depth is created purely through color blocking, overlapping typography, and grid structure.
* **Structure**: Relies heavily on visible horizontal and vertical dividers (`Divider 1`, `Divider 2`), reminiscent of newspaper columns or vintage ledger books.

**The Sensory Vibe**:

* **Sophisticated & Artisanal**: The interface radiates warmth and quality through its rich, coffee-toned dark mode and creamy light mode. It feels curated, intentional, and expensive.
* **Bold & Confident**: Massive display typography (150px+) commands attention immediately. The brand voice feels established and authoritative.
* **Tactile yet Flat**: While the design is entirely flat, the use of nostalgic borders (like zigzag lines) and warm colors evokes the feeling of physical packaging.

---

## Design Token System

### Colors (The "Artisanal Print" Palette)

**Backgrounds**:

* **Primary Dark (Canvas)**: `#382B1C` (Deep Mocha/Rich Brown). This is the signature color of the aesthetic. It is warmer and more inviting than pure black or charcoal.
* **Primary Light (Accent Canvas)**: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Cream/Alabaster). Used for contrasting sections or cards. Never use pure white.
* **Highlight Canvas**: `#D48C62` or `#C96A4B` (Muted Terracotta/Ochre). Used for bold, color-blocked accent sections to break up the dark and light.

**Foreground/Text**:

* **Text on Dark**: `#FDFBF7` (Cream). High contrast but soft on the eyes.
* **Text on Light**: `#382B1C` (Deep Mocha).
* **Muted Text**: `#A89F91` (Warm Taupe). For captions and secondary information.

**Borders & Dividers**:

* **Divider 1 / Divider 2**: Use `#FDFBF7` at 20-30% opacity on dark backgrounds, and `#382B1C` at 20-30% opacity on light backgrounds.

### Typography

**Font Selection**:

* **Display Headings**: A bold, condensed grotesque or high-contrast modern serif (e.g., *Oswald*, *Anton*, *Bebas Neue*, or *Playfair Display*). Must feel tall, impactful, and slightly vintage.
* **Body**: A clean, utilitarian sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *DM Sans*, or *Helvetica Neue*). Geometric and readable to contrast the stylistic headings.

**Hierarchy (Based on the Figma Spec)**:

* **Display Headline**: `150px` size / `80%` line-height. Massive, tight leading, often all-caps.
* **Heading 1**: `60px` size / `110%` line-height.
* **Heading 2**: `42px` size / `110%` line-height.
* **Heading 3**: `30px` size / `130%` line-height.
* **Body**: `20px` size / `139%` line-height. Generous size for comfortable reading.
* **Link/Navigation**: `26px` size / `139%` line-height.
* **Captions**: Range from `14px` to `28.8px`, often styled in all-caps with wide tracking (`tracking-widest`).

**Typography Best Practices**:

* Display headlines must have very tight line-heights (`leading-none` or `leading-[0.8]`) to emulate tight kerning in vintage print.
* Use uppercase generously for Display, H1, Links, and Captions.
* Body text should have comfortable, standard leading.

### Shapes, Radii & Borders

**The "Sharp & Clean" Rule**:

* **Border Radius**: `rounded-none` (0px) is the default. Sharp corners emphasize the editorial, print-like quality. If rounding must be used for UI conventions, keep it minimal (`rounded-sm` or 2px).
* **Dividers**: Extensive use of 1px solid borders (`border-t`, `border-b`) to separate sections, headers, and list items.
* **Vintage Accents**: Incorporate subtle vintage borders (e.g., repeating zigzag SVGs or dotted lines) specifically under hero headlines or as section breaks.

### Shadows (The Rule of Flatness)

* **No Drop Shadows**: Do not use `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`, or any soft, blurred shadows.
* **Solid Offsets (Optional)**: If depth is absolutely required for a button or card, use a solid, hard-edged offset shadow (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #382B1C`) to maintain the retro poster aesthetic, though pure flat design is preferred.

---

## Component Architecture

### 1. The Editorial Grid

* Layouts should mimic magazine spreads. Mix large spanning columns (`col-span-12`) for hero imagery with split columns (`col-span-4`, `col-span-8`) for text and supporting images.
* Use visible borders between grid columns to enhance the ledger/newspaper feel.

### 2. Buttons

* **Base Shape**: Sharp corners (`rounded-none`), tall height (`h-14` or `h-16`).
* **Primary**: Solid fill (Terracotta or Cream) with contrasting text.
* **Outline**: 1px or 2px solid border, transparent background, text matching border color.
* **Hover States**: Keep it simple. Invert the colors (background becomes text color, text becomes background color) or apply a slight opacity fade. No scaling or bouncing.

### 3. Images & Media

* Photography should feel cinematic, warm, and highly curated.
* Images should span edge-to-edge within their grid containers, with sharp `0px` border radii.
* Use color-blocking: Place a rich, warm-toned image directly next to a solid `#382B1C` block of text.

---

## Responsive Strategy

**Mobile-First Approach with Progressive Enhancement**

* **Typography Scaling**: The `150px` Display Headline must scale down significantly for mobile (e.g., to `text-6xl` or `text-7xl`), but it must retain its bold, condensed, tight-leading characteristics.
* **Layout Transformations**: Intricate multi-column editorial spreads must stack elegantly into single columns on mobile. Retain the 1px horizontal dividers between stacked sections to keep the structured print feel.
* **Padding**: Maintain generous padding even on mobile to allow the typography to breathe.

---

## Dos and Don'ts

* **DO** use massive, tightly-spaced display typography for primary headings.
* **DO** use sharp corners (`rounded-none`) for almost all UI elements.
* **DO** use 1px horizontal and vertical dividers to structure content like a newspaper or ledger.
* **DO** stick to the warm, restricted color palette (Mocha, Cream, Terracotta).
* **DON'T** use soft drop shadows, glows, or glassmorphism.
* **DON'T** use highly rounded corners or pill-shaped buttons.
* **DON'T** use vibrant neon or cool, sterile colors (like pure cyan or digital blue) unless absolutely mandated by brand guidelines.
* **DON'T** clutter the layout; allow empty space (colored in the palette's background tones) to act as a design element.
