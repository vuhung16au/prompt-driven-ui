# High-Fidelity Minimalist Gallery Design System

## Design Philosophy

**Core Concept: The White Cube**
This design system emulates a high-end contemporary art gallery. It relies on the principles of Swiss / International Typographic Style: cleanliness, readability, and objectivity. The interface is not the attraction; it is the invisible, perfectly structured frame that holds the content.

**The "Minimalist" Difference**:
Unlike generic "clean" templates, this style demands extreme discipline. It uses aggressive negative space, high-contrast monochrome palettes, and a rigid underlying grid.

* **Materiality**: Paper, ink, and glass. The design should feel like a perfectly printed, high-quality editorial magazine or an exhibition catalog.
* **Lighting**: Flat and even. There are no simulated light sources, no deep drop shadows, and no bevels. Depth is achieved through Z-index overlapping and scale, not lighting simulation.
* **Grid Architecture**: Everything snaps to an invisible grid. Alignment is paramount. Elements are either strictly left-aligned or center-aligned with profound intention.

---

## Design Token System

### Colors (The Editorial Palette)

**Background**:

* **Canvas**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for light mode) or `#252525` (Deep charcoal for dark mode). The contrast must be absolute.

**Foreground**:

* **Text (Primary)**: `#252525` (on white) or `#FFFFFF` (on charcoal).
* **Muted (Secondary)**: `#737373` (Neutral gray). Used only for metadata, captions, and secondary structural lines.

**Accents (Sparing & Vivid)**:

* **Primary Accent**: `#0055FF` (Electric Royal Blue). Used exclusively for primary action buttons, hover states on crucial links, or distinctive circular badges.
* **Gradients**: NONE. Use solid, flat colors only.

### Typography

**Font Selection**:

* **Global**: A premium Grotesque or Geometric Sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica Neue, or Roboto). It must remain neutral and highly legible.

**Hierarchy (Strict Scale)**:

* **Hero Headline**: `text-6xl sm:text-7xl md:text-8xl`, tight tracking (`tracking-tighter`), line-height 1.1.
* **Section Titles**: `text-3xl sm:text-4xl`, Medium to Semibold.
* **Body Text**: `text-base` to `text-lg`, Normal weight, relaxed leading (`leading-relaxed` or 140% line-height).
* **Captions/Labels**: `text-xs` to `text-sm` (e.g., 12/120), uppercase, wide tracking.

### Shapes & Radii

**The "Sharp" Rule**:

* **Standard Containers & Images**: `rounded-none` (0px). Sharp, 90-degree corners are the default.
* **Exception (The Badge)**: Primary action buttons or floating badges can be `rounded-full` perfect circles to create a stark contrast against the rigid rectangular grid.

### Shadows (Flat Architecture)

**1. No Shadows**:
For 95% of the interface, `box-shadow: none`.

**2. Minimal Elevation (Navigation/Modals only)**:

```css
box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05);

```

## Component Architecture

### 1. The Image Canvas (`GalleryCard`)

* **Base Styles**: `relative overflow-hidden bg-transparent`
* **Interactive States**:
* Hover: Image scales up slightly `hover:scale-105` inside its container, or a stark solid-color overlay appears with the title.
* Transition: `transition-transform duration-700 ease-out`



### 2. The Minimal Button (`Button`)

* **Base Styles**: `inline-flex items-center justify-center font-medium transition-colors duration-200 rounded-none`
* **Variants**:
* **Primary**: `bg-[#252525] text-white hover:bg-[#0055FF]`
* **Outline**: `border border-[#252525] text-[#252525] hover:bg-[#252525] hover:text-white`
* **The Badge**: `rounded-full bg-[#0055FF] text-white aspect-square flex items-center justify-center p-4`



### 3. The Structural Divider (`Divider`)

* **Base Styles**: `w-full h-px bg-[#E5E5E5]` (or a dark gray in dark mode). Used to separate sections instead of background color blocks.

## Animation System

**1. Fade In Up**:
Elements load sequentially with a subtle upward movement and fade.

```css
@keyframes fadeInUp {
  from { opacity: 0; transform: translateY(20px); }
  to { opacity: 1; transform: translateY(0); }
}

```

**2. Hover Reveal**:
Links and images should have a snappy, immediate response (duration 150-200ms) to feel highly responsive and digital.

## Layout Patterns

**1. The Asymmetric Grid**:

* Do not force symmetry. Use a 12-column grid but leave columns intentionally blank (e.g., content spanning cols 4-10, leaving 1-3 empty).

**2. Abundant Negative Space**:

* Use `py-24` or `py-32` between distinct sections. Allow content to breathe.
