# High-Fidelity Personality-Driven Editorial Design System

## Design Philosophy

**Core Concept: Structured Expressionism**
This design system marries the rigid, structured layout of a bento-box grid with the chaotic, raw expression of a personal manifesto. It is highly editorial, resembling a high-end modern art magazine or a zine more than a standard SaaS landing page.

**The Vibe**:

* **Confident & Raw**: It does not hide behind soft shadows or gentle gradients. It uses stark contrasts, massive text, and sharp borders.
* **Tactile but Flat**: Unlike Claymorphism, this style is definitively flat, relying on crisp 1px or 2px solid borders to define hierarchy, similar to Neobrutalism but more refined.
* **Personality-Forward**: The interface acts as a canvas for the creator's voice. It heavily utilizes candid cut-out photography, bold statements, and interactive micro-animations (like a custom cursor or hovering tooltips).

## Design Token System

### Colors (High-Contrast Monochrome)

**Background**:

* **Canvas**: `#292929` (Deep Charcoal) - The void that makes the content pop.
* **Card Backgrounds**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) or `#F5F5F5` (Off-White) for high contrast against the dark canvas.

**Foreground**:

* **Primary Text (on dark)**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Primary Text (on light)**: `#111111` (Near Black) for maximum legibility and starkness.
* **Muted Text**: `#737373` (Mid-Gray) - use sparingly, as this style prefers high contrast.

**Accents**:

* **Action Accent**: Use one single, highly saturated color (e.g., Electric Blue `#0055FF`, Neon Yellow `#CCFF00`, or Hot Orange `#FF4400`) strictly for primary CTAs, hover states, or highlight markers.

### Typography (The Hero)

**Font Selection**:

* **Headings**: A highly distinct Serif (like *Playfair Display* or *PP Editorial New*) OR a brutal, grotesque Sans-Serif (like *Clash Display* or *Inter Tight*).
* **Body**: A clean Sans-Serif or Monospace for technical details.

**Hierarchy (Aggressive Sizing)**:

* **Hero Headline**: `text-7xl` to `text-9xl`. It should dominate the viewport.
* **Large Paragraphs**: As seen in the Figma file, base paragraphs act as visual elements. Use sizes like `text-3xl` or `text-4xl` (`40px` base) with generous line height (`leading-[160%]`).
* **Captions/Labels**: Small, uppercase, heavily tracked (`tracking-widest`), e.g., `14/100`.

### Shapes & Borders

* **Borders**: `border-2 border-[#111111]` on light mode elements, or `border border-white/20` on dark elements.
* **Radii**: Mix sharp corners (`rounded-none`) for structural containers with perfect pills (`rounded-full`) for buttons and badges. Avoid generic `rounded-md`.

## Component Architecture

### 1. The Bento Card

* Base: `relative overflow-hidden bg-white text-[#111111] p-8 lg:p-12`
* Features: Rigid borders, internal flex layouts pushing content to corners (e.g., title top-left, arrow icon bottom-right).
* Hover: High-contrast inversion (background turns black, text turns white) or a slight scale-up of internal imagery.

### 2. Typography as Image

* Instead of icons, use massive typography to break up sections.
* Implement Marquee banners for continuous scrolling text across section dividers.

### 3. Floating Annotations

* Absolute positioned elements (stickers, handwritten SVG arrows, or asterisk badges) that break the grid and overlap two separate bento cards, adding the "personality" layer.
