## Grid, Rhythm, and Responsiveness

Desktop grid 12 columns, gap 16 px, max-width 1280 px, padding 32 px. Tablet 6 columns gap 16 px; mobile 2 columns gap 12 px and gutter 20 px. Cells with long content can span additional rows, no fixed height clipping text. Maximum of two consecutive cells of the same size if needed to create rhythm; one primary cell always maintains dominant surface area.

## Surfaces and Materials

Radius 20 px for large cells, 12 px for internal controls, do not add multiple layers of card-within-card. Border 1 px #DCDDD5; shadow 0 4 px 16 px rgba(0,0,0,.04) only on interactive cells. Up to three surface types may be used: image, solid color, and white background; all sharing the same typography set.

## Components and States

### Project Cell

The entire cell is a link if it contains only a single action. Project name and role are always visible, not only shown on hover. Focus outline 2 px sits outside the cell, not clipped by image overflow. Do not nest buttons inside links.

### Tool Cell

If there are controls inside, the cell is not a link. Buttons 44 px high with dedicated hitbox and clear label; running the tool must not automatically open the case study.

### Author Cell

Portrait does not take up more area than the main work. Caption is tied to the author's role, not self-praising quotes or fake client logos.

### Expanded Case Study

Located after the grid, spans full row. Close button has a label, returning focus properly to the originating cell; content max-width 72 ch, gallery with stable dimensions.

## Motion and Reduced Motion Strategy

Hover shifts cell 2 px over 180 ms ease-out. Do not animate the entire grid reordering when the pointer hovers over. Case study expands in 240 ms; only one case study can be open at a time. Reduced-motion does not shift position, but still changes border.

## Things to Avoid

Strictly avoid: grid where all cells are identical size, empty placeholder cells to fill space, shrinking text to fit into a row, setting layout order different from DOM order, disparate gradients on individual cells, or using Bento as an excuse to cram everything into cards.

## Pre-implementation Quality Checks

Measure contrast for actual text/surface pairs and real-world states; do not rely on isolated hex values to declare compliance. Verify complete Vietnamese diacritics support and font fallbacks.
