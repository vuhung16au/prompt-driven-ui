## 3. Minimalist Portfolio Specification

Minimalist Portfolio: Light gray background #F5F5F5, pure white surfaces, high-contrast charcoal/black text. Highly structured grid layouts, thin borders, and ample whitespace. Only a few dominant sections feature imagery (like Carousel slides); secondary content (like FAQ List Items) remains flat, text-driven, and clean. Navigation is simple and unobtrusive. Buttons have clear, high-contrast labels without relying on heavy shadows.
Crucial detail to preserve: The UI must remain "invisible." The design exists solely to frame the content (the portfolio pieces).

Detailed original style specification:

# High-Fidelity Minimalist Design System

## Design Philosophy

**Core Concept: Content as the Interface**
This design system rejects decoration. It relies on the Swiss design principles of grid alignment, strict typographic hierarchy, and extreme negative space. The interface should feel like a high-end art gallery: stark, perfectly lit, and completely subservient to the artwork it houses.

**The Sensory Vibe**:

* **Professional & Confident**: The lack of embellishment signals confidence. It does not need to shout to be heard.
* **Tactile & Crisp**: Elements feel like high-quality matte paper. Borders are crisp (1px), backgrounds are flat, and contrast is sharp.
* **Structured**: Everything aligns to a strict invisible grid. There are no floating blobs or chaotic overlaps.

---

## Design Token System

### Colors (The Monochrome Foundation)

**Background & Surfaces**:

* **Canvas Background**: `#F5F5F5` (A cool, professional light gray). Use this for the main page body to provide subtle contrast against white cards.


* **Surface/Card**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Used for content containers, carousel slides, and navigation bars to make them pop subtly against the gray canvas.

**Foreground**:

* **Text (Primary)**: `#111111` (Near Black). Maximum contrast for maximum readability.
* **Text (Secondary/Muted)**: `#666666` (Medium Gray). For metadata, secondary descriptions, and borders.

### Typography

**Font Selection**:

* **Primary Typeface**: **Inter**, **Helvetica Neue**, or **SF Pro Display**. A neo-grotesque sans-serif is non-negotiable. It provides the clean, objective feel required.

**Hierarchy**:

* **Hero Headline**: `text-5xl md:text-7xl lg:text-8xl`, `font-medium` or `font-semibold`, `tracking-tighter`.
* **Section Titles**: `text-2xl md:text-3xl`, `font-medium`, `tracking-tight`.
* **Body Text**: `text-base` to `text-lg`, `font-normal`, `leading-relaxed`.
* **Labels/Metadata**: `text-sm`, `font-medium`, `text-gray-500`, uppercase with `tracking-widest` for categories.

### Shapes & Radii

**The "Crisp Edge" Rule**:

* **Containers & Images**: `rounded-none` (0px) or at maximum `rounded-sm` (2px). No pill-shaped buttons or heavily rounded cards.
* **Borders**: Extensive use of `border` (`1px solid #E5E5E5`) to define sections instead of relying on shadows or background color changes.

### Components

**1. The Carousel Slide (Hero/Work Showcase)**:

* Full-bleed or strictly contained within the max-width grid.
* Image-heavy, relying on high-quality project photography.
* Navigation arrows should be simple icons within 1px bordered square buttons.

**2. The FAQ List Item (Accordion)**:

* Used for details, process, or contact info.
* `border-b border-gray-200` separating each item.
* Clean toggle interaction (plus/minus icon or simple arrow) that reveals text without pushing the layout awkwardly.

**3. Buttons**:

* **Primary**: `bg-black text-white hover:bg-gray-800 transition-colors`. No shadows.
* **Secondary**: `bg-transparent border border-black text-black hover:bg-gray-50`.

---

## Animation System

* **Motion Philosophy**: Snappy, functional, and minimal.
* **Hover States**: Subtle opacity shifts (e.g., image dims slightly, or text color softens) rather than physical movement. Do not use translate-y lifts.
* **Page Loads**: Simple fade-ins (`opacity-0` to `opacity-100` over 300ms).

---

## Dos and Don'ts

* **DO** use whitespace generously. If it feels like too much padding, it is probably just right.
* **DO** rely on typography size and weight to establish hierarchy, not color.
* **DON'T** use drop shadows. Rely on borders and #F5F5F5 vs #FFFFFF contrast.
* **DON'T** use colored text. Keep the palette strictly grayscale, allowing the portfolio images to provide all the color.
* **DON'T** center-align long blocks of text. Left-align for clean grid lines.

Use a consistent set of variables for colors, typography, spacing, and borders. Normal text minimum contrast 4.5:1; large text minimum 3:1. Do not truncate diacritics on uppercase letters and do not force desktop line-breaks on mobile.
