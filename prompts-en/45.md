3. Minimalist Document Specification
Minimalist Document: an ultra-clean, utility-first aesthetic inspired by printed documents and digital resumes (like Read.cv). Background is a very specific off-white/gray `#F5F5F5`, text is highly legible dark charcoal or black. The design relies entirely on typographic hierarchy, strict grid alignment, and white space rather than shadows, borders, or background colors. Elements are flat. Accents (green, orange, blue, red) are used sparingly for small tags or status indicators. On mobile, columns collapse into a single readable scroll, maintaining the document-like feel.
Crucial detail to preserve: Zero structural shadows and almost no borders. Depth is created by typography and layout, not CSS effects.

# High-Fidelity Minimalist Document Design System

## Design Philosophy

**Core Concept: The Digital Sheet of Paper**
This design system rejects decoration in favor of absolute clarity. It treats the screen as a well-typeset piece of paper. It is brutally simple, highly functional, and relies heavily on classical typographic principles. There is no room for 3D effects, gradients, or unnecessary visual noise.

**The "Document" Difference**:
Unlike traditional web design that uses heavy containers, cards, and backgrounds to separate content, this style uses whitespace and strict alignment.

* **Materiality**: Flat, matte, untextured. It should feel like reading a beautifully formatted PDF or a physical resume printed on high-quality #F5F5F5 paper.
* **Lighting**: None. The environment is entirely flat. There are no light sources, no specular highlights, and no shadows.
* **Structure over Decoration**: Hierarchy is established purely through font size, weight, and spatial grouping (margins/padding).

## Design Token System

### Colors (The "Ink and Paper" Palette)

**Background**:

* **Canvas**: `#F5F5F5` (Light gray/off-white). This is the exact canvas color, providing a softer reading experience than pure white.
* **Surface**: Pure `#FFFFFF` can be used for absolute focus areas (like a central content column), but often the background remains `#F5F5F5` throughout.

**Foreground**:

* **Text (Primary)**: `#111111` or `#000000` (Solid Black). Maximum contrast for primary reading.
* **Muted (Secondary)**: `#666666` or `#888888`. Used for dates, metadata, and secondary descriptions.

**Accents (The "Highlighter" Palette - Use Sparingly)**:

* **Utility Colors**: Pure, accessible tones for small status dots or pill tags (Green, Orange, Blue, Red).
* **Links**: Plain text, often simply underlined on hover, or a classic muted blue. No gradient buttons.

### Typography

**Font Selection**:

* **Primary Typeface**: **Inter**, **Helvetica Neue**, **SF Pro**, or similar clean, neo-grotesque sans-serif. Use a single font family for the entire interface to maintain the document aesthetic.

**Hierarchy (Based on Figma specs)**:

* **Title / Name**: `20px` size, `26px` line-height (130%). Medium or Bold weight.
* **Body**: `14px` size, `180%` line-height (approx 25px). Regular weight for maximum legibility.
* **Section Header**: `14px` size, `140%` line-height (approx 19px). Often uppercase, bold, or with slight letter-spacing (tracking) to denote categories (e.g., "WORK EXPERIENCE", "WRITING").
* **Metadata/Dates**: `12px` to `14px`, regular weight, muted color.

**Typography Best Practices**:

* Generous line-height is non-negotiable (`180%` for body).
* Left-align almost everything. Avoid center alignment except for specific intentional layouts.
* Keep line lengths comfortable (max 65-75 characters).

### Shapes & Radii

**The "Minimal Form" Rule**:

* Avatars: Perfect circles (`rounded-full`).
* Tags/Badges: Small pill shapes (`rounded-full` or `rounded-md`) with subtle backgrounds (e.g., `#EAEAEA` or a 10% opacity accent color).
* Images/Thumbnails: Sharp corners (`rounded-none`) or very minimal rounding (`rounded-sm`).
* Containers: Invisible. Do not draw boxes around content.

### Shadows & Borders

* **Shadows**: `box-shadow: none;` Do not use shadows anywhere.
* **Borders**: Extremely limited. If used, they should be hairline `1px solid #E5E5E5`. Used primarily for subtle horizontal dividers (`<hr>`) between major sections, not for wrapping elements in boxes.

---

## Component Architecture

### 1. The Layout Grid

* Use a constrained max-width container (e.g., `max-w-3xl` or `max-w-4xl`).
* Often divided into an asymmetrical grid: a narrower left column (for sticky navigation, section titles, or dates) and a wider right column (for actual content).

### 2. The List Item (Work/Writing/Projects)

* **Structure**: Flex container. Left side: Timeline/Date (Muted, smaller). Right side: Title (Bold), Subtitle/Company (Regular), Body description (Regular, relaxed leading).
* **Spacing**: Generous `margin-bottom` between items.

### 3. Buttons & Links

* **Primary Button**: Simple, solid black `#111111` background, white text, slightly rounded corners, no shadow.
* **Secondary/Ghost**: Transparent background, black text, maybe a subtle gray hover state (`hover:bg-gray-200`).
* **Inline Links**: Underlined, or standard text that underlines on hover.

---

## Responsive Strategy

**Mobile-First Approach with Progressive Enhancement**

* **Typography Scaling**: Keep sizes relatively static. A `14px` body works perfectly on mobile and desktop. Titles might scale down slightly, but the structural integrity of the text remains.
* **Layout Transformations**: Asymmetrical desktop columns (e.g., dates on the left, content on the right) must stack vertically on mobile. The date/meta should appear directly above the content it references.
* **Navigation**: Simple hamburger menu or a clean, horizontally scrolling list of text links.

---

## Dos and Don'ts

* **DO** rely entirely on whitespace to group elements.
* **DO** use strict baseline and left-alignment grids.
* **DO** maintain high contrast for all readable text.
* **DO** use an off-white/gray background like `#F5F5F5` to reduce eye strain.
* **DON'T** use dropshadows, glassmorphism, or 3D effects.
* **DON'T** put content inside outlined or shaded "cards" unless absolutely necessary for a distinct UI component.
* **DON'T** use overly large hero text (keep titles proportional, around 20px-24px).

---

## Implementation Checklist

* [ ] **Background**: Canvas `#F5F5F5`.
* [ ] **Typography**: Single sans-serif family, strict 14px body / 20px title hierarchy.
* [ ] **Line Height**: 180% leading on body text for breathability.
* [ ] **Shadows**: None. Completely flat UI.
* [ ] **Alignment**: Strict left-alignment, grid-based column offsets.
* [ ] **Accents**: Muted or entirely absent, allowing content to lead.
